I Sea Parks

Every Seattle park on one map. See what each park offers, log the ones you've visited and keep your own notes.

  • Map and list: find parks near you, or search and filter by feature.
  • Park details: address, features, photos and directions.
  • Visit tracking: log visits and watch your progress across the city's parks.
  • Private notes: remember the best picnic spot or the dog-friendly trail.
  • Help others: send a photo of a park sign to help fill in missing hours and rules.

Free, coming soon to the App Store for iPhone.
